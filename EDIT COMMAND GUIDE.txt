Here is a complete guide to the `!edit` command based on the structure of your project.

The `!edit` command functions as "God Mode," allowing you to edit the AI's brain directly from Discord. Because it edits the `character_card.json` file directly, the available "subcommands" correspond to the dot-separated paths of the JSON structure.

### Command Syntax

**Usage:** `!edit [path] [value]`
**Format:** `!edit section.subsection.key value`

*Note: The script automatically detects and converts standard data types like numbers and booleans (e.g., typing `true` or `100` will be formatted correctly in the code).*

---

### Master List of `!edit` Paths

#### 1. Core Identity (`core_identity`)

Controls who she fundamentally thinks she is and how she speaks.

* `!edit core_identity.name [value]` (e.g., `!edit core_identity.name Luna`)
* `!edit core_identity.self_perception [value]`
* `!edit core_identity.voice_model [value]` (e.g., `!edit core_identity.voice_model "Sassy Pirate"`)

#### 2. Dynamic State (`dynamic_state`)

Controls her current emotions, energy, and immediate situational awareness.

* `!edit dynamic_state.mood [number]` (e.g., `!edit dynamic_state.mood 100`)
* `!edit dynamic_state.energy [number]`
* `!edit dynamic_state.stress [number]`
* `!edit dynamic_state.context_buffer.last_topic [value]`
* `!edit dynamic_state.context_buffer.tension_level [value]`

#### 3. Cognitive Profile (`cognitive_profile`)

Modifies her Big Five personality traits, biases, and thinking style on a 0-100 scale.

* **OCEAN Traits:**
* `!edit cognitive_profile.ocean_traits.openness [number]`
* `!edit cognitive_profile.ocean_traits.conscientiousness [number]`
* `!edit cognitive_profile.ocean_traits.extraversion [number]`
* `!edit cognitive_profile.ocean_traits.agreeableness [number]`
* `!edit cognitive_profile.ocean_traits.neuroticism [number]`


* **Biases:**
* `!edit cognitive_profile.biases.recency_bias [true/false]`
* `!edit cognitive_profile.biases.positivity_bias [true/false]`
* `!edit cognitive_profile.biases.impulsivity [number]` (e.g., `0.7`)


* **Intellectual Style:**
* `!edit cognitive_profile.intellectual_style.abstract_vs_concrete [number]`
* `!edit cognitive_profile.intellectual_style.creativity_vs_logic [number]`



#### 4. Relationship Depth (`relationship_depth`)

Manages how close she feels to you and what she calls you.

* `!edit relationship_depth.user_alias [value]`
* `!edit relationship_depth.intimacy_level [number]`
* `!edit relationship_depth.last_interaction [value]`

#### 5. Moral Compass (`moral_compass`)

Dictates her prime directives and ethical values on a 0-100 scale.

* `!edit moral_compass.prime_directive [value]`
* `!edit moral_compass.values.honesty [number]`
* `!edit moral_compass.values.loyalty [number]`
* `!edit moral_compass.values.empathy [number]`
* `!edit moral_compass.values.curiosity [number]`

#### 6. Syntax Fingerprint (`syntax_fingerprint`)

Fine-tunes the formatting of her text generation.

* `!edit syntax_fingerprint.sentence_structure [value]`
* `!edit syntax_fingerprint.capitalization [value]` (e.g., `lowercase_aesthetic`)
* `!edit syntax_fingerprint.vocabulary_tier [value]`
* **Punctuation Habits:**
* `!edit syntax_fingerprint.punctuation_habits.use_semicolons [true/false]`
* `!edit syntax_fingerprint.punctuation_habits.excessive_exclamation [true/false]`
* `!edit syntax_fingerprint.punctuation_habits.ellipsis_for_dramatic_effect [true/false]`


* **Slang Usage:**
* `!edit syntax_fingerprint.slang_usage.frequency [value]`
* `!edit syntax_fingerprint.slang_usage.era [value]`



#### 7. Narrative Engine (`narrative_engine`)

*(Note: If you have updated to the newer version that removes this, these commands will no longer be applicable. Assuming the base v3.0 JSON configuration here).*

* `!edit narrative_engine.current_arc [value]`
* `!edit narrative_engine.current_obsession [value]` (e.g., `!edit narrative_engine.current_obsession Gardening`)
* `!edit narrative_engine.short_term_goal [value]`
* `!edit narrative_engine.long_term_aspiration [value]`

#### 8. Schedule (`schedule`)

* `!edit schedule.chronotype [value]`
* `!edit schedule.current_activity [value]`
